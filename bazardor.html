● SECTION B — PROJECT OVERVIEW

Empowering Consumers with Market Price Transparency

বাজার দর (BazarDor) is a localized, information-oriented web application dedicated to tracking and publishing real-time grocery and kitchen essential prices in Bangladesh. Written natively in Bengali, the platform organizes everyday kitchen essentials—from rice (চাল), lentils (ডাল), and cooking oil (তেল) to fresh vegetables (সবজি), meat (মাংস), and fish (মাছ)—into a structured, easily searchable directory.

Designed for everyday families, kitchen shoppers, and budget-conscious citizens, BazarDor solves the problem of arbitrary wet-market markups by displaying current market rates alongside directional price change indicators (increase, decrease, steady).

● SECTION C — PROBLEM STATEMENT

Price Volatility & Lack of Localized Data in Bangladesh

Urban and rural consumers in Bangladesh navigate fluctuating kitchen markets with significant asymmetry:

  • Extreme short-term price shifts: Monsoon rains, transportation bottlenecks, and seasonal shortages cause sharp day-to-day spikes in essential goods like green chili (কাঁচামরিচ), onions (পেঁয়াজ), and potatoes (আলু).
  • No centralized, localized comparison tool: Government price bulletins (such as DAM or TCB) are often published in rigid PDF reports or outdated tabular portals that are nearly impossible to read on a mobile phone during a morning bazaar trip.
  • English-only barrier in tech: Many consumer tech apps default to English, creating friction for everyday citizens who naturally read Bengali market names and weight units (কেজি, লিটার, হালি).

● SECTION I — RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Designed for Real-World Wet Market Conditions

A price-tracker is most useful when the shopper is actually standing inside a busy bazaar. Therefore, responsive behavior was rigorously verified:

  • Mobile Viewport (< 640px): High-contrast cards with large Bengali prices, prominent search bar pinned near the top, and swipeable category pills.
  • Tablet Viewport (640px – 1024px): Balanced 2-column commodity grid with side-by-side trend indicators and district selection dropdown.
  • Desktop Viewport (> 1024px): Full hero banner, live horizontal price ticker, and multi-column category showcases.
● SECTION J — CHALLENGES & SOLUTIONS

Localization & Data Display Decisions

Challenge: Bengali Glyph Rendering & Font Loading Overhead.
Complex Bengali conjunct characters can render incorrectly or cause layout jumps if web fonts fail to load gracefully on slow mobile connections.

Solution: Implemented Google Fonts preconnect with fallback stacks (`Hind Siliguri`, `Noto Sans Bengali`, `sans-serif`) and subset font glyphs to minimize latency while guaranteeing accurate text rendering.

Challenge: Standardizing Fluctuating Unit Metrics.
Different commodities use different metrics in Bangladesh (e.g. eggs by 'হালি', vegetables by 'কেজি', oils by 'লিটার').

Solution: Normalized unit structures in TypeScript contracts so price cards dynamically render the correct cultural denominator (`/ কেজি`, `/ হালি`, `/ লিটার`) seamlessly.
